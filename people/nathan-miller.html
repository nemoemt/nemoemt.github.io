About

Nathan is a student at Northwestern University studying Integrated Science, Biomedical Engineering, and Neuroscience. After working as President of NEMO in the past, Nathan has stepped into an advisory role while still assisting in operations and teaching the EMT-B course each year.

Nathan grew up in the Milwaukee area, and his deep passion for medicine shows in his work as a first responder for the Southern Ozaukee Fire Department. He has a robust background working on nonprofit leadership teams in medical settings through his work on the board of Wisconsin Care Collective and as the founder of Chicago Health Initiative. Nathan will continue his medical journey at Feinberg School of Medicine in 2027.
