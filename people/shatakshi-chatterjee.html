About

Shatakshi is a sophomore at Northwestern studying Neuroscience with a German minor, with a goal of going to medical school. As one of NEMO's External Affairs Secretaries, her goal is to expand NEMO's reach into Evanston and work with Evanston Public Library to develop a library program for kids interested in emergency medicine and medicine in general.

Outside of NEMO, Shatakshi enjoys singing and is involved in Brown Sugar A Cappella as Business Manager. She is also involved in PhiDE and enjoys being a Resident Assistant in Foster-Walker Complex. She is looking to work in EMS in the Northwest region of Illinois!
